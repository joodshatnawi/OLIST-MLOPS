
EDA Findings

1. The target variable is imbalanced, with late orders representing
   a minority class.

2. Several numerical variables show skewness and potential outliers.
   These should be handled or considered during feature engineering.

3. Temporal variables show variation in late rates across purchase
   periods, hours, and days of the week.

4. Customer geography shows differences in late rates between states.

5. The estimated delivery window provides information available at
   prediction time and is selected as a modeling feature.

6. Post-outcome variables such as delivery timestamps and review score
   are excluded to avoid data leakage.

7. ID columns are excluded because they identify individual records
   rather than representing useful predictive information.

8. The selected modeling features will be implemented in Notebook 5.
